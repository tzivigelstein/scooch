# 简体中文 (`src/copy/zh-Hans.ts`)

Simplified Chinese for mainland China, in the plain register of everyday
apps. Same rules as the Spanish guide ([../copy.md](../copy.md)) where they
apply. **Needs a review by a native speaker**: the terms below are the ones
that most need it.

## Conventions

- Full-width punctuation next to Chinese: ，。：；！？（）and “ ” for quotes
  (a name the person gave, an option of another app). A single sentence has no
  final 。; two or more end each with 。 and no space between them
  (`meta.period`, `meta.sentenceGap`).
- No space between Chinese and numbers or Latin words: 3个素材, 下载PDF,
  按Ctrl+V粘贴. A number keeps a no-break space before a unit symbol: 10 mm,
  210 × 297 mm. No space before %: 100%.
- Plurals have one form (`other`), with a measure word: {n}个素材, {n}张纸.
- Results start with 已: 已排版7个素材, 已移除photo.jpg; ongoing work with
  正在…: 正在生成PDF….
- Photo sizes by their usual names in inches (5寸, 6寸…); ID photos by their
  size in centimeters.
- Keys as printed on the keyboard (Ctrl, Shift, Enter, Delete, Esc,
  PageUp), Space as 空格; mouse actions 单击, 双击, 拖动, 滚轮.
