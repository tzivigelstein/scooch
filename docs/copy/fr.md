# Français (`src/copy/fr.ts`)

French from France, in the plain register of everyday apps (vous, imperative
for actions). Same rules as the Spanish guide ([../copy.md](../copy.md))
where they apply. **Needs a review by a native speaker.**

## Conventions

- Infinitive for buttons and actions («Télécharger le PDF», «Retirer de la
  feuille»), imperative with vous for instructions («Sélectionnez un
  élément»), past participle for results («3 éléments retirés»).
- Capital letter only on the first word.
- Typography: a no-break space (U+00A0) before «:» and inside « »; a narrow
  no-break space (U+202F) before «;», «!», «?» and «%» («100 %»). The
  parity test checks them. Typographic apostrophe ’.
- Decimal comma («2,5 cm»), a no-break space between a number and its unit
  («10 mm»); inches are «po» (pouces), as Adobe and Microsoft write them.
- Plurals: `one` is 0 and 1 («0 élément», «1 élément»), so its form says
  {n}.
- Keys as printed on French keyboards: Ctrl, Maj, Alt, Entrée, Espace,
  Suppr, Retour arrière, Échap, Pg préc, Pg suiv; on Apple devices ⌘ ⌥ ⇧ ⌃,
  said Commande, Option, Maj, Contrôle. Mouse: clic, double-clic, glisser,
  molette.
- Photo sizes in centimeters, as French photo labs name them («10 × 15»).
